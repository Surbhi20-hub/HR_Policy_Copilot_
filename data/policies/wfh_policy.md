# Work From Home (Hybrid) Policy

## 1. Eligibility
Employees who have completed probation are eligible for hybrid work. Interns must work from office unless their manager approves otherwise in writing.

## 2. Hybrid Schedule
- Default schedule: 3 days in office, 2 days remote per week.
- Tuesdays and Thursdays are mandatory office days for all teams.
- Fully remote work beyond 2 weeks requires approval from the department head and HR.

## 3. Equipment
- The company provides a laptop and a one-time home-office allowance of INR 5,000.
- Internet reimbursement: up to INR 1,000 per month against bills.

## 4. Working Hours
Core collaboration hours are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST. Employees must be reachable on Slack during these hours.

## 5. Security
Company data must only be accessed via VPN. Use of public Wi-Fi without VPN is prohibited.
