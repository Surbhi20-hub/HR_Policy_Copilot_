# Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Claim Submission
Expense claims must be submitted in the expense portal within 30 days of the expense date, with original receipts.

## 2. Travel Class
- Flights: economy class for all employees; business class only for flights over 6 hours for Director and above.
- Trains: 2AC for all employees.

## 3. Daily Allowance (Domestic)
- Metro cities: INR 2,500 per day.
- Non-metro cities: INR 1,800 per day.

## 4. Hotels
Hotel bookings must be made through the company travel desk. Self-booked hotels are reimbursed up to INR 6,000 per night in metro cities.

## 5. Non-reimbursable
Alcohol, personal entertainment, traffic fines, and upgrades are not reimbursable.
