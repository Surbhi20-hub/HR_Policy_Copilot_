# Code of Conduct

## 1. Respectful Workplace
All employees must treat colleagues, customers and vendors with respect. Discrimination based on gender, religion, caste, age, disability or sexual orientation is prohibited.

## 2. Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH)
- The company has an Internal Committee (IC) as required under the POSH Act, 2013.
- Complaints can be filed in writing to ic@company.com within 3 months of the incident.
- All complaints are treated confidentially. Retaliation against a complainant is a disciplinary offence.

## 3. Conflict of Interest
Employees must disclose any outside employment, directorship or financial interest in a vendor or competitor to HR within 15 days.

## 4. Gifts
Gifts from vendors or customers above INR 2,000 in value must be declined or declared to compliance@company.com.

## 5. Grievance Redressal
Non-POSH grievances may be raised with the reporting manager, or anonymously via the ethics hotline at ethics.company.com.
