# IT Acceptable Use Policy

## 1. Scope
Applies to all employees, interns and contractors who use company laptops, accounts, email, networks or data. Company systems are meant for work. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not affect work or break any rule in this policy.

## 2. Passwords and Multi-Factor Authentication
- Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and must be changed every 90 days.
- The last 5 passwords cannot be reused.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, VPN and the HRMS.
- An account is locked for 30 minutes after 5 failed login attempts.
- Never share your password with anyone, including IT staff and your manager.

## 3. Devices and Software
- Only software approved by IT may be installed. Send install requests to itsupport@company.com; IT replies within 2 working days.
- Personal USB drives and external hard disks must not be connected to company laptops.
- Laptops lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Do not switch this off.
- A lost or stolen device must be reported as a security incident (see Section 6).
- Company laptops must be returned to IT within 3 working days of the employee's last working day.

## 4. Network and Remote Access
- Company data must only be accessed through the company VPN, whether you are at home, at a client site or in a cafe.
- Public Wi-Fi may be used only after the VPN is connected.
- Company email on a personal phone is allowed only after the phone is registered with IT.
- Streaming, torrent downloads and online gaming on the company network are prohibited.

## 5. Email and Data Handling
- Email attachments can be up to 25 MB. Larger files must be shared through the company drive.
- Confidential data and customer data must not be sent to personal email accounts or saved on personal devices or personal cloud storage.
- Do not paste confidential or customer data into public AI chatbots or any online tool that IT has not approved.
- Automatic forwarding of company email to external addresses is not allowed.
- Links used to share files with external people must expire within 7 days.

## 6. Reporting Security Incidents and Violations
- Report phishing emails, lost or stolen devices and suspected data leaks to security@company.com within 4 hours of noticing them.
- Violations of this policy may lead to disciplinary action, up to termination of employment.
- For questions about this policy contact itsupport@company.com.
