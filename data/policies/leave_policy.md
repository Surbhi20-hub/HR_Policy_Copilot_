# Leave Policy

## 1. Scope
Applies to all full-time and probationary employees in India. Interns are covered under Section 7.

## 2. Earned / Privilege Leave (PL)
- Full-time employees accrue 18 days of PL per calendar year (1.5 days per month).
- PL can be carried forward up to a maximum of 30 days. Excess lapses on 31 December.
- PL requests of 3 or more days must be applied at least 7 days in advance.
- Encashment of up to 10 days of PL is allowed once per year in December.

## 3. Casual Leave (CL)
- 8 days per calendar year; cannot be carried forward or encashed.
- Maximum 2 consecutive days of CL at a time.

## 4. Sick Leave (SL)
- 10 days per calendar year; not encashable; carry forward up to 20 days.
- A medical certificate is required for sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days.

## 5. Maternity and Paternity Leave
- Maternity leave: 26 weeks paid, as per the Maternity Benefit Act.
- Paternity leave: 10 working days, to be availed within 3 months of childbirth or adoption.

## 6. Leave During Probation
Probationary employees accrue PL but may avail a maximum of 5 days of PL during probation.

## 7. Interns
Interns receive 1 day of leave per month of internship. Unused leave lapses at the end of the internship and is not encashable.

## 8. Contact
For questions contact hr@company.com.
