# Performance Review and Appraisal Policy

## 1. Scope and Review Cycle
- Applies to all full-time employees who have completed probation. Probationary employees and interns are covered under Section 6.
- The annual review cycle runs from 1 April to 31 March.
- A mid-year check-in is held between 1 October and 15 October. It does not carry a rating.

## 2. Rating Scale
- Ratings use a 5-point scale: 5 = Outstanding, 4 = Exceeds Expectations, 3 = Meets Expectations, 2 = Needs Improvement, 1 = Unsatisfactory.
- Ratings are proposed by the reporting manager and approved by the department head.
- No more than 10% of the employees in a department can receive a rating of 5.

## 3. Review Timeline
- Self-assessment is due by 10 April.
- Manager review must be completed by 20 April.
- The calibration meeting of managers and the department head is held by 30 April.
- The final rating is shared with the employee by 7 May.
- An employee who disagrees with the final rating can raise a dispute with hr@company.com within 7 days of receiving it. HR replies within 15 days.
- A complaint made under the Code of Conduct must never affect a rating.

## 4. Increments and Promotions
- Increment by rating: rating 5 gets 12% to 15%, rating 4 gets 8% to 11%, rating 3 gets 5% to 7%, rating 2 gets 0% to 3%, and rating 1 gets no increment.
- Increments take effect from 1 June.
- Employees who join after 31 December are reviewed but receive their first increment in the next cycle.
- To be considered for promotion, an employee needs a rating of 4 or above in 2 consecutive cycles and at least 18 months in the current role.

## 5. Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
- An employee is placed on a PIP after a rating of 1, or after a rating of 2 in two consecutive cycles.
- A PIP lasts 60 days, with a review meeting with the manager every 2 weeks.
- Written PIP goals are shared with the employee within 5 working days of the PIP start date.
- An employee on a PIP is not eligible for promotion until the PIP closes successfully.
- If the PIP is not completed successfully, it can be extended once by up to 30 days, or the employment can be ended as per the employment contract.

## 6. Probationers and Interns
- Probation lasts 6 months. A probation review is held in month 5, and the confirmation decision is shared within 7 days of that review.
- Probation can be extended once by up to 3 months.
- Probationary employees do not receive an annual rating or increment until they are confirmed.
- Interns receive a written feedback form from their manager within 5 working days of the internship end date. Interns are not rated and do not receive increments.
